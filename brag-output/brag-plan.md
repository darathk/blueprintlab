# Brag Plan: BlueprintLab (Athlete/Lifter Promo)

## 1. Core Decisions
* **Audience**: Powerlifters and strength athletes looking for premium coaching.
* **Goal**: Show them the incredible value, polish, and features they get when they sign up. Convert them from casual lifters to paying clients.
* **Hook**: "Stop Guessing. Start Executing." (Focus on intent and precision).
* **Key Features to Showcase** (Comprehensive & Extensive):
  1. **The Dashboard**: Readiness tracking, macro view.
  2. **Workout Logger**: Seamless logging, RPE, prescribed vs actual.
  3. **Direct Chat & Video**: Coach feedback integrated into the app.
  4. **Deep Analytics**: E1RM trends, fatigue tracking, history.
  5. **Meet Day Tools**: Plate loader, automated attempt strategies.
* **Outro**: "Train with BlueprintLab. Apply Now."
* **Tone**: `intense, premium, focused`
* **Duration**: ~35 seconds (extensive pacing to fit all features).
* **Visual Identity**: Dark mode #0a0a0a, glassmorphism, cyan (#06b6d4) and purple (#a855f7) accents, Inter font. Mobile-inspired UI layouts (since athletes primarily use it in the gym on their phones).

## 2. Scene Storyboard
1. **0s - 4s | The Hook**: 
   - Text: "Your Training, Elevated."
   - Visual: A sleek, dark glass dashboard fading in, pulsing with energy.
2. **4s - 10s | Intelligent Logging**:
   - Text: "Precision Workout Logging."
   - Visual: Simulating the workout screen. Prescribed sets (e.g. Squat 3x5 @ 8 RPE) vs Actual. Plate Loader popping up.
3. **10s - 16s | Direct Coaching**:
   - Text: "Real-Time Coach Feedback."
   - Visual: Chat interface sliding in. A "Video Form Check" message appearing.
4. **16s - 22s | Analytics & Progress**:
   - Text: "Track Every Metric."
   - Visual: E1RM trend line chart drawing in, fatigue gauge filling up.
5. **22s - 28s | Meet Day Execution**:
   - Text: "Automated Meet Strategy."
   - Visual: 9/9 attempt calculations, conservative/planned/reach grids.
6. **28s - 34s | Outro / CTA**:
   - Text: "Train with BlueprintLab. Apply for Coaching Today."
   - Visual: Brand logo, glowing CTA button, social proof.
